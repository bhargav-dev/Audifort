What Are The Ingredients In Audifort?

Audifort is formulated with 9 named ingredients — including Ginkgo Biloba, Vinpocetine, Bacopa Monnieri, Huperzia Serrata, L-Theanine — each listed individually below with the role it plays in the formula, so you can see exactly what you are taking before you buy.

  • 1

    Ginkgo Biloba

    CIRCULATION AND AUDITORY SUPPORT

    A long-standing herbal extract traditionally used to support healthy blood flow, including microcirculation to the small vessels that serve the ears and the brain.

  • 2

    Vinpocetine

    BLOOD FLOW AND COGNITIVE SUPPORT

    A compound derived from the periwinkle plant that is commonly included in ear-and-brain formulas for its traditional association with cerebral blood flow and mental clarity.

  • 3

    Bacopa Monnieri

    HERBAL NERVE AND MEMORY SUPPORT

    An Ayurvedic herb widely used in nootropic and hearing-wellness blends for its traditional role in supporting memory, focus and healthy nerve function.

  • 4

    Huperzia Serrata

    COGNITIVE AND NEUROTRANSMITTER SUPPORT

    A club moss extract containing huperzine A, traditionally included in brain-and-sensory formulas to support normal neurotransmitter activity and mental sharpness.

  • 5

    L-Theanine

    CALM FOCUS AND AUDITORY COMFORT

    An amino acid found in green tea that supports a relaxed but alert state, which many people find helpful when background noise or ringing feels distracting.

  • 6

    Vitamin B12

    NERVE HEALTH SUPPORT

    An essential vitamin involved in normal nerve function and red blood cell formation, both of which matter for the healthy function of the auditory system.

  • 7

    Niacin (Vitamin B3)

    CIRCULATION SUPPORT

    A B vitamin that supports normal blood flow and energy metabolism, complementing the circulation-focused herbs in the formula.

  • 8

    Zinc

    MINERAL SUPPORT FOR EAR HEALTH

    A trace mineral involved in immune function and normal cellular health, commonly included in ear-wellness formulas for its supportive role.

  • 9

    Magnesium

    MUSCLE AND NERVE COMFORT

    A mineral that supports normal nerve signalling and muscle relaxation, often included in hearing-comfort formulas for its calming effect on the body.
